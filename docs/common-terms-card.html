LB Soccer: Common Terms

One vocabulary for every Eagles coach and player. If we all use the same words, a player hears the same thing on every team, at every age.

The one idea

Get wideSpread out to the sidelines. The moment we win the ball.
Get compactPull in toward the ball so the spaces between us close. The moment we lose the ball.

Say these: cues by age

Grassroot (K–2)“Little touches, keep it close!” · “Freeze — now go the other way!” · “Great try — go again!”
Rising (3–5)“Head up — what do you see?” · “First touch into space.” · “Try your move. Good idea.”
Next XI (6–8)“Scan. Head on a swivel.” · “We lost it. Press together!” · “Possess. Keep it.”

The tactics levels

Level 1Spread out. The ball moves faster than players.Everyone plays defense.

With the ball

  • Support Move to a spot where the ball can reach you: a straight line from the ball to you, with no defender in it.
  • Check to the space Move toward the ball into space to receive it.
  • Possess (keep it) Keep the ball and move it until a gap opens.
  • Width Players near each sideline, so the defense has to stretch.
  • Depth One player high for the ball through, and one behind the ball so we can switch.
  • Go long Play over the top into the space behind the other team.
  • Bounce pass Pass into a teammate's feet and get it straight back.
  • Up, back, through Up to a forward, back to a supporting player, through to a runner going past.
  • Forward, square, forward Forward, then sideways to a teammate, then forward again from the new angle.

Without the ball

  • Press / Press together Go win the ball back right away, as a group.
  • Pressure The first defender's job. Get close to the ball, slow the attacker down, stay on your feet.
  • Cover The second defender's job. Stand behind and to the side of the first defender, ready if they get beaten.
  • Balance Everyone else's job. Tuck in toward the middle to protect the space behind the first two.
  • Strong side The side of the field the ball is on.
  • Weak side The side of the field away from the ball. Weak-side players tuck in.
  • Goal side Between the player you are marking and our goal.
  • Free player The defender who isn't marking anyone and covers the others.
  • Swoosh The shape of the back line: the strong-side back up pressuring, the center backs behind in cover, the weak-side back tucked in.

Calls

  • Man on A defender is tight on you. Protect the ball or play it back.
  • Turn / Open The same call. You have space: receive the ball and face forward.
  • Time Nobody is near you. Take a touch and look up.
  • Through Play the ball into the space behind the defenders for a runner.
  • Switch Move the ball to the other side of the field.
  • Square I am level with you. Pass sideways.
  • “Keeper!” The keeper is coming for the ball. Everyone else, get out of the way.
  • “Away!” Clear it big and far, out of danger. No short pass.
  • “Step!” Back line, step up together toward the ball.
  • “Drop!” Back line, drop off together toward our goal.

Positions

  • Keeper Guards the goal and organizes the back line. The only player who can use their hands, and only inside the box.
  • Backs Our defenders, the line nearest our goal.
  • Outside back A back on the left or right. Defends the wide attacker, and joins the attack down the sideline when we get wide.
  • Center back A back in the middle. Defends the space in front of goal, and is usually the cover when the outside back pressures.
  • Mids Our midfielders, linking the backs to the forwards. At K–5 this is the only name we use.
  • The 6 Holding mid, 6–8. Sits in front of the backs, wins the ball back and starts our attacks.
  • The 8 Box-to-box mid, 6–8. Helps defend and joins the attack, and covers the most ground.
  • The 10 Attacking mid, 6–8. Plays behind the striker, between the lines, and makes the final pass.
  • Forwards Our attackers, the line nearest their goal.
  • Winger A forward on the left or right. Stays wide to give us width, and takes players on.
  • Striker The forward in the middle, highest up the field. Holds the ball up and finishes.

Moments

  • Transition The moment the ball changes teams.
  • Counter Attacking fast after we win it, before the other team gets compact.
  • Rest defense The two or three players who stay back when we attack, ready for the counter.
  • The extra player When we have more players than them near the ball, somebody is free.
  • Build, don't just boot it Play out from the back with passes.
  • Play the score Up a goal: get compact and keep it.

On the field

  • Thirds The field in three parts: defending third, middle third, attacking third.
  • Between the lines The space between two lines of defenders, where a player can receive the ball and turn.
  • Build-out line At U10, the line the other team must stay behind until our keeper's pass is received.

Restarts

  • Throw-in Both feet on the ground, on or behind the line. Two hands, and the ball comes from behind your head.
  • Goal kick Short to a back and build out with passes. At U10, the other team stays behind the build-out line until the pass is received.
  • Kick-off Tap it back to a mid (the 6 at 6–8), who plays it out wide.
  • Free kick Direct: you can score straight from it. Indirect: it has to touch another player before it goes in, and the referee keeps an arm up until it does.
  • The wall When we defend a free kick near our goal, players stand side by side between the ball and the goal, as far back as your division's Black Swamp guidelines say. The keeper sets it.

Habits and skills

  • Scan Look around before the ball arrives, so you know your next move.
  • Head on a swivel Keep scanning the whole time, with and without the ball.
  • First touch into space Your first touch moves the ball away from pressure, toward where you're going next.
  • Little touches Keep the ball close while you dribble.
  • Shield Put your body between the defender and the ball.
  • Take them on Dribble at a defender and try to beat them with a move.
